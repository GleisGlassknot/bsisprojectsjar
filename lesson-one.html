[LESSON 1] Solving Systems of Linear Equations


This lesson teaches how to solve systems of linear equations by defining variables, substituting, simplifying, and solving for the unknowns.



[Problem 1]

The sum of two integers is 76 and their difference is 2.
What are the two numbers?

GIVEN:
x + y = 76,
x - y = 2

Step 1:
Define y in terms of x.
Let y = x + 2

Step 2:
Substitute newly defined y into the first equation.
x + (x + 2) = 76

Step 3:
Combine like terms.
2x + 2 = 76

Step 4:
Isolate the term with x by subtracting 2 from both sides.
2x + 2 - 2 = 76 - 2

Step 5:
Simplify & Solve for x.
2x = 74
2x/2 = 74/2

[ x = 37 ]

Step 6:
Solve for y using x.
y = x + 2
y = 37 + 2

[ y = 39 ]


[Problem 2]

The average of two quizzes is 85. The first quiz is 10 points higher than the second. Find the value of the quizzes.

GIVEN:
x + y / 2 = 85,
x + 10 = y

Step 1:
Define y in terms of x.
Let y = x + 10

Step 2:
Substitute newly defined y into the first equation.
x + (x + 10) / 2 = 85

Step 3:
Combine like terms.
2x + 10 / 2 = 85

Step 4:
Simplify by cancelling out and dividing by 2.
x + 5 = 85

Step 5:
Isolate the term with x by subtracting 5 from both sides.
x + 5 - 5 = 85 - 5

[ x = 80 ]

Step 6:
Solve for y using x.
y = x + 10
y = 80 + 10

[ y = 90 ]



[Problem 3]

The sum of two numbers is 150. If the larger number is divided by the smaller number the quotient is 4.
Find the two numbers.

GIVEN:
x + y = 150,
x / y = 4

Step 1:
Define x in terms of y.
Let x = 4y

Step 2:
Substitute newly defined x into the first equation.
4y + y = 150

Step 3:
Combine like terms.
5y = 150

Step 4:
Simplify by dividing by 5 on both sides.
5y / 5 = 150 / 5

[ y = 30 ]

Step 5:
Solve for x using y.
x = 4y
x = 4(30)

[ x = 120 ]



[Problem 4]

When 7 is added to three times a number the sum is 34.
Find the number.

GIVEN:
3x + 7 = 34

Step 1:
Simplify by subtracting 7 on both sides.
3x + 7 - 7 = 34 - 7
3x = 27

Step 2:
Isolate the term with x by dividing by 3 on both sides.
3x / 3 = 27 / 3

[ x = 9 ]

Step 3:
Confirm Results.
3(9) + 7 = 34
27 + 7 = 34
34 = 34
